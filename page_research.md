1. Header with nav

3. Subtitle: "Adventure, elegance, and a splash of seaside breeze"
4. Search widget: From (Kuwait, KWI) — To (Sochi, AER) — Date — Passengers — Search arrow
5. Breadcrumb: Home / Book Flights / Flights To Sochi
6. Hero image of the city
7. Content sections about the destination (attractions, places to visit)
8. Footer
